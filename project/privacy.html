Privacy Policy

How Rosée Skin collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you shop with us.

Last updated: September 2026

Who We Are

This website is operated by Rosée Skin ("we," "us," or "our"). Rosée Skin is an online beauty store that provides authentic Korean skincare products, including products from Medicube, to customers in Egypt.

Our goal is to make Korean skincare more accessible by providing products locally, so customers can shop without having to deal directly with international shipping, customs procedures, or import fees.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit or make a purchase through our website.

Information We Collect

We may collect information in the following ways:

  • Information you provide: Your name, phone number, email address, delivery address, and order details when you place an order or contact us.
  • Contact information: Information you provide when you contact us through our contact form, email, or other available communication channels.
  • Order information: Details about the products you purchase, your order status, and delivery information.
  • Automatically collected information: We may collect information about how you interact with our website, including your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and time spent on the website.
  • Cookies: We may use cookies and similar technologies to improve website functionality, remember your preferences, and understand how visitors use our website.

How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information to:

  • Process and fulfill your orders
  • Arrange delivery of your products within Egypt
  • Contact you regarding your order or delivery
  • Respond to your questions and customer support requests
  • Improve our products, services, and website experience
  • Manage your account or shopping preferences, where applicable
  • Send promotional offers or updates if you have chosen to receive them
  • Prevent fraudulent or unauthorized activity
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Payment Information

When you make a purchase, payment information may be processed through secure third-party payment providers.

Rosée Skin does not intentionally store complete payment card details on its own servers. Payment information is handled according to the security and privacy practices of the payment provider used for your transaction.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies for purposes such as:

  • Essential cookies: Required for basic website functionality, shopping cart features, and order processing.
  • Preference cookies: Help remember your preferences and improve your browsing experience.
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand how visitors use our website so we can improve the site and its content.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

  • Order information: Retained as necessary to process orders, provide customer support, and maintain appropriate business records.
  • Contact information: Retained for as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry or provide related support.
  • Newsletter information: Kept until you unsubscribe or request that we remove your information.
  • Website and analytics data: May be retained for a reasonable period to help us understand website performance and improve our services.

When information is no longer needed, we may securely delete or anonymize it.

Sharing Your Information

To provide our services, we may share limited personal information with trusted service providers when necessary.

For example, your name, phone number, and delivery address may be shared with delivery or logistics providers so that your order can be delivered to you.

We may also share information when required by law, legal proceedings, or governmental authorities.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their independent marketing purposes.

Your Rights

Depending on applicable laws and your location, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • Access: Request information about the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information where legally applicable.
  • Objection: Object to certain uses of your personal information.
  • Portability: Request your personal information in a structured format where applicable.

To exercise any applicable rights or ask questions about your personal information, please contact us through our contact page or the contact information provided on our website.

International Products & Delivery

Rosée Skin provides Korean skincare products to customers in Egypt.

When products are sourced internationally, certain information may be processed by suppliers, logistics providers, or other service providers involved in sourcing and delivering products to Rosée Skin.

We aim to make the purchasing process easier for our customers by handling the relevant importing and delivery arrangements rather than requiring customers to manage international shipping or customs procedures themselves.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

These measures may include:

  • Secure transmission of information
  • Restricted access to personal information
  • Secure handling of customer and order data
  • Regular review of our website and security practices

However, no online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended for children under the age of 16.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child without appropriate authorization, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, website, or applicable requirements.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we handle your personal information.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, your personal information, or how we handle your data, please contact Rosée Skin through our contact page or the contact information provided on our website.
